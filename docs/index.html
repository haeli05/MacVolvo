READY WHEN YOU ARE

From install
to your library.

MacVolvo prepares a private Wine environment and uses DXMT to translate Direct3D 11 graphics to Metal.

  1. Get MacVolvo.

    Visit GitHub Releases. Check the download’s signing status, follow its installation notes, and move the app to Applications.

  2. Set up Steam.

    Open MacVolvo and follow setup. Sign in to Steam with your own account, then install CS2 or Deadlock.

  3. Choose your game.

    Pick a resolution and press Play. Your launch options also appear in Steam Properties after launching through MacVolvo.

Before you start.

An Apple Silicon Mac, macOS Tahoe 26.5 or later, Rosetta, a Steam account, and an internet connection. Allow roughly 70 GB per game. We recommend at least 16 GB of memory. Intel Macs are not supported.

A WORK IN PROGRESS

Known issues.
Practical fixes.

Known launch, audio, performance and Deadlock UI issues remain in version 1.1.

Black screen with sound on launch

Quit the game completely, then launch it again. This can happen from MacVolvo or Steam. A captured fullscreen deadlock is fixed; intermittent black screens remain under investigation.

Audio static or crackling

Open Audio MIDI Setup → Window → Show Audio Devices. Select your output device and set Format to 96,000 Hz (96 kHz), if supported. Quit and restart the game afterward.

This helped on the tested M2 Max. MacVolvo does not change your system audio settings. Apple’s audio-device guide ↗

CS2 lag spikes during combat

Persistent combat spikes and severe FPS drops are still being investigated. Initial shader compilation can cause stutters, but repeated spikes should not automatically be attributed to it.

Deadlock camera stops after a match

If clicks work but moving the mouse no longer turns the camera, quit and restart the game. Post-match recovery is not yet fully verified.

Deadlock UI shifts after changing video settings

This remains under investigation. Close Deadlock, turn off its Fullscreen option in MacVolvo and relaunch at a resolution that fits your display before trying the in-game settings again.
